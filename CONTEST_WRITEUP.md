# 🎯 STAGE SET — September Pipeline Accelerator

## Field Sales Contest | September 1–30, 2026

---

## Executive Summary

**STAGE SET** is a month-long pipeline acceleration contest designed to incentivize funnel progression across the entire Field Sales organization. Unlike traditional build contests that reward volume, STAGE SET rewards **movement** — pushing deals through stages toward close.

This contest directly addresses our biggest conversion gap: **56% of GPV entering Discovery dies there.** By weighting points toward later-stage progression and up-market deal sizes, we align rep behavior with what actually drives Q4 revenue.

**Created by:** Nate Obermark & Gloria Pena  
**Duration:** September 1–30, 2026  
**Participants:** All Field Sale managers, region-wide  
**Prize:** Bragging rights + leaderboard glory  
**Tracking:** Live dashboard updated daily at 9:00 AM PT from SFDC data

---

## Why This Contest, Why Now

Based on current cycle times:
- A heavy late-Q3 build push lands revenue in **late Q1** (too far out)
- Deals unblocked in Negotiation today land in **December** (Q4 revenue)
- Deals reaching Mutual Close Plan in September land in **late October** (Q4 revenue)

**The insight:** Late-funnel movement in September is what actually pays in Q4. Top-of-funnel volume is already covered by GPA. This contest fills the gap by incentivizing the conversion work that turns pipe into revenue.

---

## Scoring System

### Base Points (per qualifying deal)

| Action | Points |
|--------|--------|
| New Opportunity Created (≥$500K GPV) | **1 pt** |
| Opportunity moved into Evaluation | **2 pts** |
| Opportunity moved into Negotiation (Stage 5) | **3 pts** |

### Up-Market Multipliers

| Deal Size (GPV) | Multiplier |
|-----------------|-----------|
| $500K – $999K | 1x (base) |
| $1M – $1.99M | **2x** |
| $2M+ | **3x** |

### Scoring Examples

| Scenario | Calculation | Points Earned |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| Create a $600K opp | 1 × 1x | **1 pt** |
| Move a $750K opp into Evaluation | 2 × 1x | **2 pts** |
| Move a $750K opp into Negotiation | 3 × 1x | **3 pts** |
| Create a $1.2M opp | 1 × 2x | **2 pts** |
| Move a $1.2M opp into Negotiation | 3 × 2x | **6 pts** |
| Create a $2.5M opp | 1 × 3x | **3 pts** |
| Move a $2.5M opp into Evaluation | 2 × 3x | **6 pts** |
| Move a $2.5M opp into Negotiation | 3 × 3x | **9 pts** |
| **Full journey on a $2.5M deal** | (1+2+3) × 3x | **18 pts total** |

---

## Eligibility Rules

1. **Minimum deal size:** $500K GPV (anything below does not count)
2. **All stage movements during September count** — including deals already in Evaluation before Sept 1 that move to Negotiation during the month
3. **All Field Sale managers participate** — individual manager leaderboard (not team/region)
4. **Stage movement must occur within September 1–30, 2026** (based on SFDC stage change date)

---

## Why This Works

### Addresses the Discovery Graveyard
By awarding only 1 point for creation but 3 points for reaching Negotiation, managers are incentivized to **work existing pipe** — not just stuff the top of funnel.

### Rewards Up-Market Pursuit
The 2x/3x multipliers for $1M+ and $2M+ deals ensure managers prioritize the large opportunities that move the needle on quota attainment.

### Aligns with Q4 Revenue
Deals reaching Negotiation in September are the ones most likely to close in Q4. This contest directly accelerates the pipe that pays.

### Simple Enough to Drive Behavior
Managers can do the math in their heads: "If I push this $1.5M deal from Eval to Negotiation, that's 6 points." Simple scoring = clear motivation.

### Complements GPA (Not Competes)
GPA already covers top-of-funnel activity. This contest layers on top by rewarding the conversion work that GPA doesn't directly incentivize.
