# 🎯 PIPELINE POINTS — Field Pipeline Challenge

## Field Sales Contest | September 2026

---

## Executive Summary

**PIPELINE POINTS** is a month-long pipeline creation contest designed to accelerate AE self-sourced pipeline, build more meaningful GPV, and drive action through friendly competition.

**The problem:** We're behind on leads, creating H2 NVA risk. We need reps leaning into self-sourced pipeline — and not just any pipeline, but qualified SQLs with real GPV attached.

**The solution:** A points-based system that rewards creating qualified self-sourced opportunities, running SAMPS demos, hitting weekly targets, and competing for team-level weekly crowns — all rolling into one org-wide leaderboard.

**Created by:** Nate Obermark & Gloria Pena  
**Duration:** September 2026 (4 weeks)  
**Participants:** All Field Sale AEs  
**Prize Pool:** $1,000 (1st: $500, 2nd: $300, 3rd: $200)  
**Tracking:** Live dashboard updated daily at 9:00 AM PT from SFDC data

---

## Why This Contest, Why Now

We're behind on leads heading into H2. Self-sourced pipeline is the lever we can pull immediately. This contest is designed to:

1. **Accelerate self-sourced pipeline creation** — reps own their destiny
2. **Build meaningful GPV** — bigger swings earn more points
3. **Drive quality behavior** — SAMPS demos and weekly consistency are rewarded
4. **Create friendly competition** — team-level weekly winners + org-wide leaderboard

---

## Scoring System

### 🎯 Create Qualified Pipeline

Points are earned when a new self-sourced opportunity becomes an SQL (Stage 2+ = genuine seller conversation has occurred):

| SQL Opportunity Size | Points |
|---------------------|--------|
| $500K – $999K GPV | **+1 pt** |
| $1M – $1.99M GPV | **+2 pts** |
| $2M+ GPV | **+3 pts** |

**Bigger swings = bigger points.**

### 📈 Build & Progress

| Action | Points |
|--------|--------|
| SAMPS demo call held | **+2 pts** |
| Hit your weekly SQL GPV target | **+3 pts** |
| Finish #1 in self-sourced SQL GPV on your team that week | **+5 pts** |

### 📊 Weekly SQL GPV Targets

| Tier | Weekly Target |
|------|--------------|
| Tier 1 | $4.5M |
| Tier 2 | $4.0M |
| Tier 3 | $3.5M |

This keeps the competition fair across territories while holding everyone accountable to meaningful weekly pipeline creation.

---

## How Points Stack

A rep can accumulate points from multiple sources each week:

| Source | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Create a $2M+ SQL | +3 pts |
| Run a SAMPS demo | +2 pts |
| Hit weekly GPV target | +3 pts |
| Finish #1 on team | +5 pts |
| **Total for one great week** | **13 pts** |

Points stack week over week across the full month.

---

## Competition Structure

### One Org-Wide Leaderboard
All points roll into a single Field leaderboard. Everyone competes nationally.

### Weekly Team Crowns
Every week, the **#1 self-sourced SQL GPV builder on each team** earns a +5 point bonus. This creates local competition within each manager's team while feeding the national leaderboard.

### Prizes

| Place | Prize |
|-------|-------|
| 🥇 1st | $500 |
| 🥈 2nd | $300 |
| 🥉 3rd | $200 |

---

## What We're Incentivizing

The contest rewards both **consistency** and **ambition**:

- **SQL creation** = repeatable points (do it every week)
- **Upmarket SQL creation** = more points (lean into big opportunities)
- **SAMPS demos** = quality behavior (real conversations, not just pipe stuffing)
- **Weekly GPV target** = consistency (hold yourself accountable)
- **#1 team SQL GPV** = competitive accelerator (win your team, earn bonus)

When you find a $1M or $2M+ opportunity, **lean in**. Those are the opportunities that can materially change a territory, and the scoring reflects that.

---

## Eligibility & Rules

1. **Self-sourced only** — opportunities must be AE-generated (not inbound/BDR-sourced)
2. **SQL = Stage 2+** — a genuine seller conversation must have occurred
3. **Minimum deal size:** $500K GPV
4. **SAMPS demos must be logged** in SFDC to count
5. **Weekly targets based on tier assignment** — targets keep competition fair across territories
6. **All Field Sale AEs participate** — one org-wide leaderboard

---

## Tracking & Visibility

- **Live leaderboard website** — updated daily at 9:00 AM PT
- **Data source:** SFDC opportunity data (self-sourced SQLs, stage dates, GPV)
- **Automated refresh:** Data pulls run automatically each morning
- **Weekly Slack updates:** Team crowns and top performers called out
